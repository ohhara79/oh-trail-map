# A 3D view: orbit, walk, and trail playback

## Context

The map was 2D only, and the goal is to walk around the mountain and look around. A **3D**
button now switches the map area to a MapLibre GL view with terrain. It has three modes:

- **Orbit**: tilt and rotate from above.
- **Walk**: eye height, following the ground.
- **Playback**: a trail's ▶ plays its route at eye height.

On a phone there is a joystick, drag to look, and an optional gyroscope. Leaflet stays the default
view, and everything stays keyless and client-side.

Facts this rests on (checked against `maplibre-gl` 6.9.0 and the tile hosts):
- **Terrain source.** Terrain is AWS Terrain Tiles (terrarium, z15, CORS `*`). Around Seoul this is
  SRTM at about 30 m, so shapes are right, but there are no cliffs or trees. The three basemaps all
  send CORS `*` and drape over it.
- **Draping.** `line`, `fill` and `raster` layers drape onto the terrain. `circle` layers sit at
  terrain height instead.
- **Eye-height camera.** It is placed with `calculateCameraOptionsFromTo` and a fixed 40 m look-at
  distance.
  - `calculateCameraOptionsFromCameraLngLatAltRotation` falls back to a 10 km centre near level
    views.
  - MapLibre derives the near clip plane from that distance, so a 10 km centre puts it about 130 m
    out, hiding the ground underfoot.
  - At 40 m it is about 0.75 m, at zoom ≈ 20.3.
  - Walk mode also needs `maxPitch 179`, `maxZoom 24` and `centerClampedToGround false`.
- **Terrain height.** `queryTerrainElevation` returns metres above sea level. Exaggeration stays at
  1, so eye height is real.

## Change

1. **Dependency and config.**
   - `maplibre-gl@^6.9.0` is added as a dependency.
   - `vite.config.ts` gets `worker.format: 'es'`, because MapLibre starts a module worker, loaded
     via `?worker&url`.
   - All MapLibre code is behind `import('./view3d')`. A 2D visit downloads none of it: the chunk is
     about 1 MB, plus a 0.5 MB worker.
2. **New modules.**
   - `geo.ts`: `offset`, `bearing`, and `angleDelta`, which moved out of `map.ts`.
   - `scene3d.ts`: the style, sources, and filter/opacity expressions.
     - The expressions mirror `mapOpacityOf` and the `HALO_RINGS` z-order.
     - Line widths are screen pixels from above and fixed metres on the ground: a 1 m trail and
       2.6 m / 1.8 m casings.
   - `view3d.ts`: owns the map and the mode switch (handlers, pitch and zoom limits, FOV, fog). It
     also handles:
     - clicks: points first, then trails, with the same select/deselect rule as 2D
     - the location marker
     - capture-phase Escape: playback → walk → orbit
   - `firstPerson.ts`: one rAF loop. It computes view direction = base (trail tangent, or phone
     orientation) + your own turning.
     - Ground height is smoothed with τ 0.15 s.
     - Entering walk descends from the orbit camera's height, capped at 600 m.
   - `walkControls.ts`: turns input into intent.
     - Keys are read by `e.code`, so movement still works with the Korean IME on.
     - A mouse click captures the pointer (pointer lock); drag looks around.
     - Joystick and look-drag use separate `pointerId`s, so both work at once.
     - `deviceorientation` α/β/γ is converted to yaw and look.
   - `trailPlayback.ts`: the trail as a distance-parameterised path, plus play/pause/speed state
     (1×, 5×, 20×, 50× of 1.4 m/s).
   - `hud3d.ts`: binds the HUD in `index.html`. CSS shows each part per `data-mode3d` on `#app`.
3. **Existing files.**
   - `main.ts`:
     - `open3d()` / `close3d()` hand the view over by centre and zoom (MapLibre zoom = Leaflet − 1).
     - Existing single paths gain one line each: `restyleAll` → `syncTrails`, plus basemap, points,
       zoom-to, locate/follow and heading.
     - A trail's ▶ makes the trail visible, selects it and plays it.
     - With no `WebGL2RenderingContext`, the button is dimmed and explains why.
   - `ui.ts`: gains the 3D button, the row ▶ and `closeDrawer()`.
   - `map.ts`: exports `LOCATE_ICON_HTML` and adds `onHeading`.
   - `points.ts`: exports `popupContent` and the pin colours.
   - `selection.ts`: exports `tolerance` and `HALO_RINGS`.
   - `gpx.ts`: exports `EARTH_RADIUS`.
   - `index.html` / `style.css`:
     - `#stage` holds `#map` and `#map3d`, which swap by `visibility` so Leaflet keeps its size.
     - `#view3d` sits top-right, and `#compass` moves below it.
     - In 3D, `#locate` rises to clear MapLibre's taller control.

## Verification

1. `npm run build`: the typecheck passes. The main `index-*.js` has no MapLibre; `view3d-*.js`,
   `view3d-*.css` and `maplibre-gl-worker-*.js` are separate.
2. Headless Chrome (SwiftShader), desktop 1280×800:
   - **2D and entering 3D**
     - A 2D load makes no MapLibre or terrarium requests.
     - 3D opens at the same centre, and terrarium tiles load.
   - **Walk**
     - It lands at 1.7 m (camera zoom 20.34).
     - Dragging turns and tilts; looking down shows ground.
     - W moves forward; Esc → orbit.
   - **Playback and eye height**
     - A row ▶ enters playback with the HUD bar.
     - Eye heights 1.7 / 20 / 80 m all render.
   - **Sync and close**
     - Satellite drapes.
     - A point click opens its popup.
     - Selecting or hiding a trail in the list updates the 3D filters.
     - Closing 3D returns to 2D.
3. Pixel 7 emulation:
   - The HUD fits between ☰ and 3D.
   - The joystick moves you.
   - A ▶ from the drawer closes it and plays.
