# Lift the 3D orbit dots off the ground

## Context

In 3D orbit, the national point dots and the profile cursor dot sometimes flickered. It looked
like z-fighting, and nearly was. MapLibre's circle shader puts a dot's centre exactly on the DEM
height, then compares that depth with the terrain depth texture using a margin of `0.0001`
(`calculate_visibility` in `_prelude.vertex.glsl`). The terrain mesh is coarser than the DEM
sample the centre stands on, so the centre sits right on the edge. As the camera moved, the dot
flipped between solid and faded.

MapLibre 6.9 has no style property that raises a circle. `circle-translate` moves it on screen
only, and symbols use the same check. To lift the dots, we draw them ourselves.

## Change

### `src/pointDots.ts` (new)

A custom `'3d'` layer, `LAYER_POINT_DOTS`, built like `pointBalls.ts`: the same origin-in-metres
trick, the same matrix, and `queryTerrainElevation` every frame.

- It draws one screen-facing quad per dot, with a disc and a ring drawn in the shader. The sizes
  and colours are the old circles': the points, the hover ring behind a point, and the profile
  cursor dot.
- **Lift:** the centre is at `ground + max(2 m, 3 px of ground)`. Pixels per metre come from
  projecting a metre east and a metre south of the spot, so the lift looks the same at every
  zoom. Up close it is still enough in metres to clear the coarse mesh.
- The whole disc is drawn at its centre's depth, so a ridge in front hides it and the slope it
  stands on never cuts it.
- It tests depth but does not write it. The dots overlap in draw order, as the circles did: the
  hover ring, then unnamed points, then named points, then the cursor.

### `src/scene3d.ts`

- `LAYER_POINTS` stays for `queryRenderedFeatures` clicks and hover, with zero opacity.
- `LAYER_POINT_HOVER`, `LAYER_PROFILE_CURSOR` and `hoveredPointFilter` are gone.
  `SRC_PROFILE_CURSOR` now holds only the walking shadow disc.

### `src/view3d.ts`

- Adds the dots layer after the balls. `syncGround` shows it in orbit only, in place of
  `LAYER_PROFILE_CURSOR`.
- Hover calls `dots.setHover`, and `setProfileCursor` calls `dots.setCursor`.

## Trade-offs

- When the camera is tilted, a dot sits a few pixels above its spot. Seen from straight above,
  it does not move.
- A dot whose terrain tile has not loaded is not drawn yet, the same as the balls.

## Verification

1. `npm run build` passes.
2. `npm run dev`, 3D orbit over steep ground: tilt, orbit and zoom. The dots stay solid, a ridge
   in front still hides them, named dots draw above unnamed ones, hover shows the ring, and a
   click still opens the popup. Hiding points in the panel hides their dots.
3. With a trail selected and the profile open, the cursor dot is steady in orbit. Walking shows
   balls and shadows only, and playback shows neither. 2D is unchanged.
